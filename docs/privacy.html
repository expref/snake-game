Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

Your privacy matters to us. Snake & Multiply ("the Game") is built to be played entirely on your device.

1. Data we store

The Game stores your preferences (sound, music, voice, vibration), your best scores, and a randomly generated local User ID. All of this is kept on your device only.

2. No account required

You can play without signing in. The optional local profile name you enter is stored only on your device.

3. No tracking

We do not collect analytics, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

4. No third parties

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. The Game does not send your data off your device.

5. Children

The Game is designed to be family friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your control

You can clear all stored data at any time using "Reset progress" in Settings, or by uninstalling the Game.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email experiencereflectgrow@gmail.com.
