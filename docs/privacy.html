Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026 · com.expref.snakemultiply

Your privacy matters to us. In short: Snake & Multiply ("the App") collects no personal data — everything stays on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no servers and requires no account. It stores the following only on your device, and never sends it to us or anyone else: your preferences (music, sound, voice, vibration), your best scores and progress, an optional display name you choose, and a randomly generated local User ID (e.g. "TITU-AB12CD") that is not linked to your identity.

2. No account or sign-in required

You can use the entire App without registering. The optional "login" simply saves a display name locally for a personalized greeting — it does not create an account on any server.

3. No tracking, ads, or analytics

We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies of any kind. We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, location, contacts, photos, microphone input, or usage analytics.

4. Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files. It uses your device's built-in text-to-speech and audio only to play in-game speech and sounds; no audio is recorded or transmitted.

5. No data sharing or selling

Because we collect no personal data, we have nothing to sell or share, and we do not transfer any data to third parties.

6. Children's privacy

Snake & Multiply is a family-friendly educational game suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no chat or social features. It is designed to comply with the Google Play Families policy and with children's privacy laws including COPPA (US) and GDPR-K (EU).

7. Your control

All stored data lives on your device. You can erase it at any time using "Reset progress" in Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email experiencereflectgrow@gmail.com.
